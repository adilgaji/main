Training hard but not growing? You might be making three *recovery* mistakes. Training makes *tiny tears* in your muscles. Repair cells sew them up, like patching a shirt. Muscles grow back *stronger*. Mistake one: *short sleep*. In deep sleep, you release growth hormone, a repair messenger. Fix: sleep seven to eight hours in a *dark, cool room*. Mistake two: skipping *protein*, your muscle's building blocks. Fix: eat protein foods, like eggs, fish, or beans, around training. Then spread protein across your meals.
