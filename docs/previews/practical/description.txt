Training makes tiny tears in your muscles, and repair cells patch them up so they grow back stronger. Here are three common recovery mistakes: short sleep, skipping protein, and an ice bath right after lifting (early research). Each one comes with a simple fix. #Shorts #musclerecovery #fitness

⚠️ Educational, not medical advice. Talk to a doctor about your own health.
