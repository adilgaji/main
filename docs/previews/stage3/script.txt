Your muscles don't grow in the gym. They grow during *recovery*. Hard training can cause tiny fiber damage. Immune cells called *macrophages* can clear debris. Then *satellite cells*, muscle stem cells, wake and fuse into fibers to repair them. *Protein building* stays elevated for up to two days. Soreness from *lactic acid*? Largely a *myth*. Early research suggests one sleepless night slows protein building. So eat protein-rich meals, and *sleep*. Strength is built at rest.
