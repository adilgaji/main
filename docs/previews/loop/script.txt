Heart pounding before a big test? Let's follow the signal inside you. First, your brain senses *danger*. A tiny *fear alarm* in your brain goes off. It pings your brain's *control center*. The signal races down nerves to your *adrenal glands*. They sit on top of your kidneys. They pour a *hormone*, a fast body message, into your blood. Your heart beats faster. Your airways open wider, so more air gets in. Blood rushes to your muscles. Your stomach takes a break. Your skin sweats to cool you down.
