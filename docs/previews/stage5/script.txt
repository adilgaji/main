Your muscles don't really grow while you lift. They grow while you *rest*. Hard training can make tiny tears in muscle. Then a *repair crew* shows up. Among the first are *cleanup cells*. They eat the broken bits. Next, *sleeping repair cells* on the muscle wake up. They split into more cells. Then they join the muscle to patch it. Muscle *building* stays high for up to two days. Many think sore muscles the next day come from acid made during exercise. That's mostly a *myth*. Early research suggests one night with no sleep slows muscle building. So eat meals with *protein*, your body's building blocks. And *sleep* well. Your strength is built at rest.
